About MOSGUARD

At MOSGUARD, we believe that nature can provide useful solutions to everyday challenges. MOSGUARD is a mosquito repellent lotion developed using lemongrass as its main active ingredient. Our product was created as part of a scientific investigation into the potential mosquito-repellent properties of lemongrass. Our goal is to develop a simple, affordable and convenient plant-based mosquito repellent that can be used during outdoor activities and in areas where mosquitoes are common. We combine science, nature and innovation to explore a practical approach to mosquito protection.

OUR MISSION

To provide a nature-inspired mosquito repellent option while promoting the exploration and use of locally available plant resources.

OUR VISION

To become a trusted plant-based mosquito-repellent brand that provides accessible and innovative products inspired by nature.
